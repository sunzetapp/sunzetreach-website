Privacy Policy

SunzetReach

SunzetReach ("SunzetReach", "we", "us", or "our") is a service created to make it easy to send small moments of kindness, encouragement and connection through anonymous and personal "Little Lights" (Lysglimt).

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what happens to your information when you use the SunzetReach app and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use SunzetReach, we may collect or process the following information:

Account information

If you create an account, we may process information such as:

  • Email address
  • Account identifier
  • SunzetReach-ID
  • Profile information you choose to provide
  • Language or other preferences associated with your account

SunzetReach may also allow you to use certain parts of the service as a guest.

SunzetReach-ID

SunzetReach uses a unique SunzetReach-ID to allow users to connect without requiring them to share their telephone number.

Your SunzetReach-ID may be used to identify your account within the SunzetReach service and to establish connections with other users.

Little Lights and messages

When you send a Little Light, we may store information necessary to deliver and manage it, including:

  • The Little Light itself
  • The type of message or card
  • The recipient or destination, where applicable
  • The date and time it was sent
  • Information needed to deliver or forward the Little Light

World Lights are designed to be anonymous. A World Light may continue through the SunzetReach system without publicly identifying the person who originally sent it.

Technical information

We may process technical information needed to operate and secure the service, such as account identifiers, authentication information, device-related information, error information and service logs.

We do not use this information to sell personal profiles or personal information to third parties.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide and operate SunzetReach
  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Allow users to connect using SunzetReach-ID
  • Send and deliver Little Lights
  • Forward World Lights through the service
  • Schedule World Lights for delivery
  • Maintain the user's history and received content
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve reliability, security and functionality
  • Detect and prevent misuse of the service
  • Process purchases or subscriptions where applicable
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

3. World Lights and Anonymity

A central part of SunzetReach is the ability to send an anonymous Little Light into the world.

When a World Light is sent, the system may retain the information necessary for that Light to continue its journey, including its message, journey information, delivery information and destination information.

A World Light is not intended to publicly identify its original sender.

4. What Happens When You Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your SunzetReach account.

When your account is deleted, personal account information and the connection between your account and the service will be removed or anonymized as appropriate.

World Lights that you have already sent may continue to exist and travel through the SunzetReach system after your account has been deleted.

This is an intentional part of the World Light system. A World Light is designed to continue its journey independently of the account that originally sent it.

Your deleted account will therefore not be used to identify you as the sender of an existing anonymous World Light.

Information that must be retained for legal, security or technical reasons may be retained for as long as necessary for those purposes.

5. Data Sharing and Service Providers

We may use trusted service providers to operate SunzetReach. These providers may process information on our behalf and only to the extent necessary to provide the relevant service.

SunzetReach uses Supabase for backend services such as authentication, database functionality and secure application infrastructure.

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not share personal information with third parties for their own advertising purposes.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through SunzetReach.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. You should use a strong password and keep your account credentials private.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including rights to:

  • Access information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Request a copy of certain information
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or where necessary for legitimate security or technical purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

SunzetReach is not intended for children who are below the minimum age required to use the service under applicable law.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable legal requirements.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

9. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The SunzetReach app does not use cookies in the same way as a traditional website.

Our website may use basic technologies necessary to operate the website and understand general website usage.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as SunzetReach develops or legal requirements change.

If we make material changes, we will update the date below and provide additional notice where appropriate.
